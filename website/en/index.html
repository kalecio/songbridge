THE BRIDGE IS YOURS.

We take care of the player.
You choose the way.

Songbridge began with a simple idea: your collection deserves a space of its own. A desktop app dedicated to the music you keep and the stories it carries.

Built with Tauri, React, and Rust. Developed in the open, with room for new ideas — including yours.
